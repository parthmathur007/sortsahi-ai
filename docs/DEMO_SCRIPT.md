# SortSahi AI — 3-Minute Demo Script

*For WeMakeDevs Environmental Hacks 2026 submission video*

---

## 0:00 — Hook (15 seconds)

"India generates 62 million tonnes of waste every year. Yet in most households,
only about 30% is correctly segregated. The reason? People simply don't know
which bin an item belongs to."

*Show: a kitchen counter with mixed waste — a banana peel next to a battery next to a pizza box*

---

## 0:15 — Introduce SortSahi AI (15 seconds)

"SortSahi AI solves this. It identifies your household waste and tells you
exactly which bin it belongs to — instantly, for free, no app download required."

*Show: the SortSahi AI home page open on a mobile browser*

---

## 0:30 — Text Search Demo (25 seconds)

"Let's start with the simplest use case. I type 'banana peel'..."

*Type "banana peel" in the search box, click Classify*

"Immediately: Wet Waste. Green bin. With a full explanation and disposal steps,
based on the Solid Waste Management Rules 2016."

*Show the result card with waste stream badge, explanation, and disposal steps*

"Now let me try something trickier — 'pizza box'..."

*Type "pizza box", classify*

"SortSahi asks: is the box clean and dry, or greasy from food? This is the
right question. The correct answer actually changes the bin."

*Click "Food-greasy or soaked" option to show conditional guidance*

---

## 1:00 — Image Upload Demo (30 seconds)

"Now the image feature. I'll upload a photo of this old battery."

*Upload a battery photo or demonstrate the interface*

"With a configured AI API key, SortSahi uses Google Gemini Vision to identify
the item from the photo. It then cross-references our reviewed knowledge base —
the AI identifies, our rules guide."

*Show the Special-Care result for the battery*

"Notice: AI image analysis is always paired with our verified knowledge base.
The AI never invents disposal rules."

---

## 1:30 — Daily Challenge (30 seconds)

"SortSahi also teaches. The daily challenge shows one question from our
curated bank of 30 waste-sorting questions."

*Navigate to Daily Challenge*

"Today's question: Where do used wet wipes go? People often flush them — wrong!
They block drains. The correct answer is the Sanitary bin."

*Select the right answer, show the feedback and explanation*

"Correct! +10 GreenPoints earned. Points carry toward your GreenLevel."

---

## 2:00 — GreenScore & Levels (20 seconds)

"The GreenLevels system has five tiers: Green Beginner all the way to
Planet Protector at 1,000 points. The system rewards learning, not
repetitive scanning."

*Navigate to My Progress page, show the score card and level roadmap*

---

## 2:20 — AWS Integration (20 seconds)

"SortSahi is deployed on AWS Amplify. The Next.js API route that handles
image classification runs server-side as a Lambda function — the AI API key
is never exposed to the browser. Deployment is one GitHub push."

*Show: amplify.yml, briefly mention the architecture*

---

## 2:40 — Environmental Value (20 seconds)

"Every incorrectly segregated item creates problems: recyclables contaminate
compost, hazardous items enter landfills. SortSahi addresses the moment of
decision — when someone is standing at their bins and isn't sure.

Better knowledge → better sorting → less contamination → more recycling.
That's the environmental value."

---

## Closing (10 seconds)

"SortSahi AI. Identify it. Sort it right.
Built by ASAP for WeMakeDevs Environmental Hacks 2026."

*Show home page*
